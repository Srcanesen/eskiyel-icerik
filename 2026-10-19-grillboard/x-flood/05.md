How it works: the agent and the board talk through plain files in a session folder. server.py serves that folder on 127.0.0.1 only, and the board re-reads it about once a second. No build step, no packages. The server only talks to your own machine; the agent and its research may still reach outside services, as they always do.

The interview method is @mattpocockuk's grilling skill (MIT). The board, the sketches, the references and the research are mine.
